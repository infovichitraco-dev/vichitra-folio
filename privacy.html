Privacy

How we measure visits

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: we count visits to understand which pages help people, and to make the site better. We never learn your name from your visit, we don't sell anything about you, and we don't use it to follow you around the internet.

What we measure

  • Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager): how many people visit, roughly where from (city level), what device and browser, how they found us (for example Google search or Instagram), which pages and sections they reach, and which buttons they tap, such as “WhatsApp us”.
  • Microsoft Clarity: anonymous recordings of how a visit scrolls and taps, and heatmaps of where people click, so we can spot parts of the site that confuse people. Text you type into the site’s demos is hidden from these recordings.
  • Google Search Console: the words people searched on Google before finding us. This is about Google searches in general, not about you.

What we don’t collect

  • Your name, phone number or email, unless you choose to message or email us yourself.
  • Anything you type into the demos on our service pages. Those stay in your browser.
  • Payment details of any kind. The site doesn’t take payments.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity use small cookies to tell a new visit from a returning one. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the site will still work.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp or email us, we receive what you send, use it only to reply and work with you, and don’t share it with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Block cookies or use a private window, and visits won’t be linked together.
  • Google offers a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to namaste@vichitraco.com.
