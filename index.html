Hamari Katha

Two friends.
One table.

This did not start as an agency. It started as lunch. Scroll through the years with us.

2018

Two best friends from college, one shared love for food. The Food Therapy is born and Mumbai starts eating with us.

2020

Reels arrive on Instagram. What was a hobby gets serious. The camera work gets sharper, the storytelling gets braver.

2022

Brands start sliding into our DMs. Can you do this for us? One client leads to another. And another.

Jan 2025

Priyanka quits her job and goes all in. No safety net, full conviction.

Mar 2025

Vichitra Co is incorporated. The agency is official, and the work speaks louder than the paperwork.

Aur ab?

A play cafe we launched was fully packed within three weeks of opening. And right now we are about to launch a jewellery brand online. The story is still being written.
