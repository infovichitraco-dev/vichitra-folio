Questions

Things people usually ask.

Do you make the calls and send the messages for us?

We can run the outreach for you, or set everything up and train your team to run it. Many clients start with us running it, then take it in-house.

Isn’t cold outreach just spam?

Not when it’s done properly. We write to a small, well-researched list, with a real reason to reach out, follow the rules for contacting people, and stop when someone says no.

Which channels do you use?

Whatever your buyers actually use: email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, phone or in-person visits. Often a mix.

Do we need a CRM?

A simple one helps a lot. We set one up that fits how you work, or use what you already have.

Can you help us hire salespeople?

Yes. We write the job description, help with interviews, and train new hires once they join.

How much does it cost?

It depends on whether we run the outreach or train your team, and for how long. We quote after a short chat. Message us on WhatsApp.
