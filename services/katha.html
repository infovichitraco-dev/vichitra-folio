Questions

Things people usually ask.

Which platforms do you handle?

Mostly Instagram, plus Facebook, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn where your customers actually are. We’d rather do two platforms well than five badly.

Do you shoot the content too?

Yes. We can plan a shoot day with our Drishti team, or work with photos and videos your team takes on a phone. We send simple shot lists so it’s easy.

Can I approve posts before they go out?

Always. We share each week’s posts on WhatsApp, you approve or ask for changes, and only then do we schedule them.

Will you reply to comments and DMs?

If you want us to. We agree on a reply guide first, and anything sensitive, like complaints or big orders, comes straight to you.

Can you promise a number of followers?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. We promise a steady plan, good content and an honest monthly report on what it’s doing for your business.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many platforms and how much content you need. We quote after a short chat. Message us on WhatsApp.
