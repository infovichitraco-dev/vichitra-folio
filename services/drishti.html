    Questions

    Things people usually ask.

    Where do you shoot?

    At a studio, at your place or on location, whatever the brief needs. We plan it with you before the shoot day.

    Do you provide models, props and styling?

    Yes, we can arrange them as part of the plan, or work with your team and products. Everything is agreed before the shoot.

    Are AI images realistic? Is it honest?

    We start from real photos of your real product, so the label, shape and colours stay accurate. We always tell you which images used AI.

    Can you shoot for Amazon and quick-commerce apps?

    Yes. We shoot to each platform’s image rules and deliver the sizes they ask for, along with your website and social crops.

    Can you fly a drone anywhere?

    Not everywhere. Some areas need permission or are no-fly zones. We check the location and plan within the rules before we book anything.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the number of shots, the location and the edit. We quote after a short chat. Message us on WhatsApp.
