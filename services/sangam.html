    Questions

    Things people usually ask.

    Big creators or small ones?

    Usually a mix. Small, local creators often have more loyal audiences and cost less. Big ones bring reach. We pick based on your goal, not follower counts.

    How do you spot fake followers?

    We look at engagement patterns, the comments, where the audience is from, and the creator’s past brand work. Anything odd, we leave out.

    Are creator fees part of your fee?

    No. Creator fees are separate and agreed with you before anyone is booked. You see exactly who gets paid what.

    Can we use the creators’ videos in our ads?

    Yes, if usage rights are agreed in the contract. We sort that out upfront so there are no surprises later.

    Do creators have to label paid posts?

    Yes. India’s advertising guidelines require clear disclosure of paid partnerships. We make sure every post follows them.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the number of creators and the campaign length. We quote after a short chat. Message us on WhatsApp.
