Questions

Things people usually ask.

Shopify or a custom website?

It depends on what you sell and how you work. Shopify is great for most stores. A custom build makes sense when you need something specific. We’ll recommend one after our chat, and explain why.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We set it up so you can change text, photos and products yourself, and show you how. We’re around for bigger changes.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes, we can set up both, in your name, so you always own them.

Can you set up Amazon, Flipkart or quick-commerce listings?

We help with listings, images and product copy, and guide you through each platform’s onboarding. Approval is up to the platform.

How long does a website take?

A landing page is quicker than a full store with many products. You’ll get a clear timeline after our first chat.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the pages, features and products. We quote after a short chat. Message us on WhatsApp.
