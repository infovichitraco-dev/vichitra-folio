Admissions season

The brief

Questions

Things people usually ask.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your goal and what a customer is worth to you. We suggest a sensible test budget after our first chat, and only scale when the numbers say so.

Is the ad budget part of your fee?

No. Ad money goes from your account straight to Meta, Google or the marketplace. Our fee is separate, and you always see exactly what was spent.

Can you guarantee results?

No honest agency can. We guarantee proper tracking, steady testing and a weekly report that tells you the truth, including when something isn’t working.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. We set them up in your business’s name and work with access you give us.

Do you make the ads too?

Yes. Our Katha and Drishti teams make the reels, carousels and images, so the ads are made to be tested, not just to look nice.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the platforms and how much we manage. We quote after a short chat. Message us on WhatsApp.
