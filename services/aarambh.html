Sample work

How we think, shown on three made-up brands.

Concepts made to show our process. These are not client projects.
Concept · Cloud kitchen

Masala Box

The brief

A home-style Mumbai cloud kitchen that should feel like the dabba from home, not one more delivery app.

The idea

Built around the steel tiffin: turmeric and chilli colours, a warm handwritten-feeling serif, and a stamp like the ones on old ration cards.

Concept · Tea stall chain

Chai Sutra

The brief

Three busy tea stalls want to become a chain young people photograph, without losing the cutting-chai crowd.

The idea

Every kulhad tells a small Mumbai story. A playful Devanagari-style display face, a blob mark that's never quite the same twice, and colours from a rainy-day tapri.

Concept · Neighbourhood gym

Pehlwan Fitness

The brief

A 20-year-old akhada-style gym is going premium and needs to stand next to the big chains without pretending to be one.

The idea

Keep the proud old name, make it loud. Heavy capitals, one electric colour on black, and a square mark that works on a kettlebell, a T-shirt and a membership card.

Questions

Things people usually ask.

Do I need a name before we start?

No. Naming is one of the six things Aarambh covers. Come with just the idea and we'll find the name together.

Can you just make a logo?

Yes. We'll still ask a few questions first, about who you sell to and what makes you different, so the logo fits the business and not just the moment.

How long does it take?

It depends on how much we're making. A logo and identity is quicker than a full brand with naming and packaging. You'll get a clear timeline after our first chat.

What will I get at the end?

Every final file in the formats you need for print, web and social, plus a brand guidebook so your team, your printer or your next agency can use it correctly.

Do you work with businesses outside Mumbai?

Yes. Most of the work happens over calls and WhatsApp, so we work with brands across India.

How much does it cost?

Every brand needs something different, so we quote after a short chat about what you need. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.
