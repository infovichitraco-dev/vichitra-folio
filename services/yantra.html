    Questions

    Things people usually ask.

    Do we own what you build?

    Yes. You own the software and the data, and we hand over access and documentation.

    Is our data safe?

    We keep data in accounts you control wherever possible, give each person only the access they need, and follow India’s data-protection rules.

    Will the chatbot say wrong things?

    It answers only from information you approve, and hands the chat to a person when it isn’t sure. You can read every conversation.

    Can it work with software we already use?

    Often, yes. Many tools can be connected. We check what you use before we promise anything.

    What happens after launch?

    We train your team, fix what needs fixing and keep improving it with you. Software is never really “done”.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on what we build. Starting small keeps it affordable. We quote after a short chat. Message us on WhatsApp.
